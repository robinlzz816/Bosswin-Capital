Bosswin Research · Market Views

CIO Interview
2024 · 04 · Shanghai Securities News
Li Zhizhong, Bosswin Capital: Riding the Tech Wave — Seizing Opportunities of the Era
In an exclusive interview with Shanghai Securities News, Li Zhizhong, Chairman of Bosswin Capital, outlined his investment philosophy and market outlook. Drawing on Sun Tzu's wisdom that "a skilled warrior seeks advantage from the situation, not from individuals," Li argues that investment strategies must continuously evolve alongside the times. After 15+ years in China's securities industry — spanning IT research at CAICT, sell-side research, asset management, and founding Bosswin — Li has built a three-pillar investment framework: assess industry momentum, understand competitive dynamics, and evaluate valuation. His core conviction: "fish where the fish are." In China's ongoing economic transformation, Li sees technology — particularly AI — as the defining investment theme of the decade. In the near term, compute infrastructure stands to benefit most directly from AI adoption; over the medium-to-long term, large language models and downstream applications are expected to generate a new generation of leading companies.
